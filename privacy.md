# Veezynk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23-November-2026

Veezynk ("we", "us") is a mobile app that helps creators see their combined, de-duplicated audience across social media platforms. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## What we collect
- **Account details:** your email, username and password (stored securely; we cannot see your password).
- **Content you add:** captions and photos you post through Veezynk.
- **Connected accounts:** if you choose to connect Instagram, Facebook or TikTok, we receive only what you approve on that platform's permission screen. This can include your account name, follower counts, your posts, and comments and activity on your own content.
- **Usage data:** basic information about how the app is used, to fix bugs and improve it.

## How we use it
- To show you your follower totals, engagement and notifications in one place.
- To estimate your unique audience by removing duplicate followers and engagement across platforms.
- To operate, secure and improve Veezynk.

We **do not sell** your data, and we do not use it to sell you to advertisers without your consent.

## Who we share it with
Service providers that run the app on our behalf, such as our database and hosting provider (Supabase) and the platforms you choose to connect (Meta and TikTok). Each handles data under its own terms.

## Your choices
- You can disconnect any platform at any time inside the app. We then stop accessing that platform.
- You can ask us to delete your account and data (see below).

## Data deletion
To delete your data, email YOUR_EMAIL with the subject "Delete my data" from the email address on your account. We will delete your account and the data linked to it within 30 days. You can also remove Veezynk's access from your Instagram, Facebook or TikTok settings.

## Security
We use access controls and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

## Children
Veezynk is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

## Changes
We will update this page when this policy changes.
